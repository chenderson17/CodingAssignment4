Kale Salad Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch of fresh kale
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 small cucumber
  • 1 carrot
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash the kale thoroughly.
  2. Remove the stems and chop the leaves.
  3. Slice the cucumber and carrot.
  4. Place the vegetables in a large bowl.
  5. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, and honey.
  6. Pour the dressing over the vegetables.
  7. Toss everything together.
  8. Season with salt and pepper.
  9. Serve and enjoy!
